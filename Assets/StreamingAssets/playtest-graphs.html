03 / WHERE DOES PROGRESS DROP?

Checkpoint reach rate

First arrivals ÷ starts in that level. Only observed markers appear; a missing marker is not a measured zero.

Deaths and manual restarts

Each outcome ÷ all starts. Quitting and leaving a level are not deaths. Deaths are coral; restarts are amber.
