01 / CAN PLAYERS FINISH?

Completion rate

Completed attempts ÷ all started attempts. A closed or unfinished attempt stays in the denominator.

02 / HOW LONG IS A SUCCESSFUL RUN?

Active completion time

Median active seconds among completed attempts. Pause and background time are excluded.

03 / WHERE DOES PROGRESS DROP?

Checkpoint reach rate

Attempts reaching a marker ÷ starts in that level. Only observed markers appear; missing markers are not measured zeros.

04 / HOW OFTEN DOES A RUN BREAK DOWN?

Deaths and manual restarts

Each outcome ÷ started attempts in that level. Quits and leaving the level are separate from deaths.

Sample sizes matter

Small samples suggest where to watch the next playtest. They do not prove why a player struggled. Repeated attempts can come from one person; completion time excludes unsuccessful runs.
